Online Trainings

Greenheck's online courses touch on a variety of HVAC subjects and can be taken at your leisure. You can earn Professional Development Hours (PDH) for completing Fan Fundamentals courses 1.1, 1.2 and Fan Fundamentals-Motors. We will send you a certificate of completion for your PDH, or your personal files. You must have an account in order for us to record your attendance and send certificates.
